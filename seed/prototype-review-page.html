Sudan event audit: spot-check

771 CLEAR events, 20–27 Oct 2026 (dev). Claude labelled each event's kind and flagged 299 as duplicates of an incident already represented by an earlier event. Your job is to agree or disagree. Judgments save in this browser as you go; use Export when done and paste the result back to Claude.

What to judge. Each card is one real-world incident, situation or campaign as Claude sees it, with every event CLEAR created for it. Cards were cut by Claude from the summaries, not by CLEAR; the Location column is CLEAR's resolved place and is itself often wrong (a state or "Sudan" when the text names a town), so judge by the place names in the summary. Orange rows are the ones Claude called duplicates (the first report in a cluster is not a duplicate). Answer one question per card: should these be one event? Tick not this one on any row that is a genuinely different incident. Rows show Claude's short summary of the event, not the original text; day, time (UTC), source and location are from CLEAR.
Duplicates that did not fit a cluster (64). Claude called each of these a restatement of an earlier event. Mark whether you believe that from the summary alone; if you cannot tell, leave it.
Events where CLEAR did merge signals (60 of 771). The question here is the opposite one: did it merge things that belong together? Known over-merges include "Protests in Khartoum" (7 unrelated protests over 5 days) and the Aj Jabalain dog-attack event.
Kind labels, random sample of 40 non-duplicate events. inc = discrete incident · sit = ongoing situation (prices, fuel, shortages) · camp = member of a multi-location campaign · rep = report, roundup, statistic or aid-delivery note · mil = military-on-military or strategic · noise = out of scope.
